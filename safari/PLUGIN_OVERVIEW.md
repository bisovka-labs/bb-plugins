Let your agents use Safari: the real one, with your tabs and logins.

## What you get

- Agents list your tabs, open pages in new tabs, read text or HTML, click
  links and buttons, fill forms, run JavaScript, and take screenshots.
- A `bb safari` command for the terminal.

## Setup

Allow bb to control Safari when macOS asks. To let agents click and type,
turn on Safari → Settings → Developer → «Allow JavaScript from Apple
Events» (enable «Show features for web developers» under Advanced first).

Everything runs locally through AppleScript; nothing leaves the Mac.
